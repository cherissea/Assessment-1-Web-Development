Characters

Peter Pevensie

The eldest sibling and The High King who led the battle representing responsibility and courage

Susan Pevensie

The Gentle Queen. She became the legendary archer known for her grace and caution. Armed with her magical bow and a horn that summons help in times of need, she serves as a protective guardian of her family

Edmund Pevensie

The Redeemed. Initially lured by the White Witch's promises, he learns the true cost of greed. After being saved by Aslan, he becomes a loyal and wise king, representing forgiveness and justice

Lucy Pevensie

The youngest sibling She is the first sibling to discover the portal to narnia, her faith and pure heart allows her to see the magic of narnia before anyone else.

Aslan

The Great Lion and True King of Narnia. A powerful, wise, and compassionate authority, who serves as a mentor to the children and makes pivotal sacrifice to save Narnia

Jadis

The white witch. A cold and poweful sorceress who has usurped the throne of Narnia. She cursed the land to a century of winter.
